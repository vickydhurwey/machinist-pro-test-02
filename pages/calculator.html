⚙️ Machinist Formulas

Machine tools और machining calculations

01. Spindle Speed (RPM)

N = (1000 × V) / (π × D)

02. Cutting Speed

V = (π × D × N) / 1000

03. Feed Rate

F = f × N

04. Machining Time

T = L / (f × N)

05. Depth of Cut

d = (D₁ − D₂) / 2

06. Material Removal Rate

MRR = V × f × d

07. Taper Ratio

Taper = (D − d) / L

08. Gear Ratio

Ratio = Teeth of Driven / Teeth of Driver

⚡ Electrician Formulas

Basic Electrical और Ohm's Law calculations

01. Ohm's Law

V = I × R

02. Electric Current

I = V / R

03. Resistance

R = V / I

04. Electrical Power

P = V × I

05. Electrical Energy

E = P × t

06. Series Resistance

R = R₁ + R₂ + R₃

07. Parallel Resistance (2 Resistors)

R = (R₁ × R₂) / (R₁ + R₂)

08. Power in Kilowatt

kW = W / 1000

📐 Workshop Calculation & Science

Mathematics, Geometry और Basic Science

01. Circle Area

A = π × r²

02. Circle Circumference

C = 2 × π × r

03. Rectangle Area

A = Length × Breadth

04. Square Area

A = Side²

05. Triangle Area

A = ½ × Base × Height

06. Cylinder Volume

V = π × r² × h

07. Percentage

Percentage = (Part / Total) × 100

08. Speed

Speed = Distance / Time

09. Density

Density = Mass / Volume

10. Work

W = Force × Distance

11. Mechanical Power

P = Work / Time

12. Average

Average = Sum / Number of Values

13. Millimeter to Inch

Inch = mm / 25.4

14. Inch to Millimeter

mm = Inch × 25.4
